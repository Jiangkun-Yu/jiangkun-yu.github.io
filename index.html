About

I am a doctoral researcher specializing in robotic micromanipulation, soft robotics, and electromagnetic actuation systems. My research focuses on developing high-speed and high-precision robotic systems for microscale manipulation.

My work combines robotics, control, mechanics, and rapid prototyping, with strong emphasis on practical implementation and experimental validation.
